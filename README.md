# Submivo

Submivo takes product details from one form, creates accounts on directories, launch sites and profile pages, submits the product and handles confirmation emails. It is free during beta.

## About

Submivo is a web service for submitting a product to directories, launch sites and profile pages. Product owners provide their website, descriptions, categories and images in one form. The service signs up on the supported sites, submits the product and handles confirmation emails.
